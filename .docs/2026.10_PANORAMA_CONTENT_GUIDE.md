# Panorama Wallpapers with ComfyUI — Content Guide

**Created:** 2026-10-14 | **For:** the Panorama background mode (one wide image across every seat, see `RECENT_UPDATES.md` 2026-10-14)

Goal: one big wallpaper that runs across all screens of the room, with hints to **BiBaBu** hidden in it, and GIFs
(cats, logos) riding across it as a scene layer.

> Model names and settings reflect what was current around mid-2026, checked against the web on 2026-10-14.
> ComfyUI moves fast: when a node or model has an **official ComfyUI template** (Workflow → Browse Templates),
> start from it and keep its defaults for the first run.

---

## 1. Know your target size first

WaBiBaBuSy stretches the panorama over the whole room canvas, and each screen shows its slice. Distortion only
appears if the picture has a different shape (aspect) from the canvas.

1. Open the Scene editor → **Background** tab → Mode **Panorama (spans all screens)**.
2. The hint under the image reads e.g. **"Sharpest at 19,200 × 1,080 px (this room's canvas)"**. That is your target.
   - 10 × 1920 monitors with no gaps = 19,200 × 1,080. Bezel / table gaps from the topology make it wider.
   - **Ring with two rows:** the canvas also contains the *turn gaps* (default 150 cm between the rows). The part of the
     picture inside a turn gap is never shown. Use the room preview to see which stretches land on screens, and keep
     logos and faces out of the gaps.
3. Aim for that **aspect ratio** while generating, and hit the exact size at the upscale step (§4.5).

Rules for the picture itself:

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Keep the **horizon at the same height** everywhere (e.g. 60 % down) | The eye reads 10 panels as one image only if the horizon lines up |
| **Calm middle band** (sky, road, river, space) | That's where the GIF layer travels; busy backgrounds hide the sprites |
| BiBaBu text in the **upper-middle third**, not at the edges | Taskbar and desktop icons cover the bottom and left |
| For a **Ring**, the right edge must continue into the left edge | The sprite path wraps; the picture should too (§4.4) |
| No transparency, JPG or PNG | Auto-suggest only recognises opaque stills as panoramas |

## 2. Models

| Job | Model | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Base panels, fast iterations** | **Z-Image Turbo** (`z_image_turbo_bf16.safetensors`, text encoder `qwen_3_4b.safetensors` with *Load CLIP* type `lumina2`, VAE `ae.safetensors`) | Official template: **8 steps, CFG 1, sampler `res_multistep`, scheduler `simple`**. At CFG 1 the negative prompt does nothing. Renders short quoted text ("BiBaBu") well. Best around 1 MP; going straight to 2K can distort. |
| Higher quality / more control | **Z-Image Base** (non-distilled) | 28–50 steps, CFG ~3–5, negative prompts work. Slower; use for the hero panel if Turbo looks flat. |
| **Outpainting / continuing panels** | **FLUX.1 Fill [dev]** (`flux1-fill-dev`, T5-XXL + CLIP-L, `ae` VAE) | The best-documented outpainting model. Use `FluxGuidance` **30**, ~20–28 steps. The full model wants ~24 GB VRAM; use an fp8/GGUF version on smaller cards. |
| Outpainting, lighter alternative | **FLUX.2 Klein** (4B) edit / inpaint / outpaint workflows | Newer and lighter. Worth a side-by-side test against Flux Fill on your GPU. |
| Text and logos, style-consistent edits | **Qwen-Image (2512)** / **Qwen-Image-Edit** | Strongest open model for lettering; Edit is good for "put a BiBaBu sign on this building" without regenerating the panel. |
| Quick ring-seamless test images | **Qwen 360 Diffusion** LoRA (on Qwen-Image), or **DiT360** (Flux path) via the `ComfyUI-DiT360Plus` custom nodes | Generates 360° equirectangular 2:1 images (2048 × 1024) whose left and right edges meet. Low resolution for 10 screens, but perfect for testing the Ring. |
| Hidden lettering in the landscape | SDXL + **ControlNet QR Code Monster v2**, or a Union ControlNet for Flux / Z-Image | Turns a black-and-white "BiBaBu" mask into ridges, clouds or city lights (§5). |
| Upscaling | **4x-UltraSharp** (`Upscale Image (using Model)`), then **Ultimate SD Upscale** with Z-Image Turbo at denoise 0.2–0.3 | The first step adds pixels, the second adds detail. Alternative: **SeedVR2**. |

Prompting Z-Image: write **natural sentences**, describe the scene from back to front, and put any text in
quotes: `a pink neon sign that reads "BiBaBu"`. Longer, concrete prompts beat keyword lists.

## 3. Two ways to build the strip

### Route A — Quick test: 360° panorama (30 minutes)

Use this to try out the Panorama mode, the Ring wrap and the GIF layer before investing in real artwork.

1. Generate a 2048 × 1024 equirectangular image with Qwen 360 Diffusion or DiT360. Add "seamless 360 equirectangular
   panorama, matching left and right edges" to the prompt.
2. **Crop the middle band.** The top and bottom of an equirectangular image are stretched; the middle is not.
   Crop 2048 × ~115 for a 10-screen row (aspect 17.8 : 1, matching 19,200 × 1,080).
3. Upscale ×4 with 4x-UltraSharp, then resize to the target width. That is ~9× in total, so expect it to look very
   soft. It is good for checking the Ring wrap and the GIF layer, not for the party.

### Route B — The real thing: outpainting chain (an evening per wallpaper)

> **Use the ready-made Klein workflow in §8 instead** (12 GB VRAM, one Run, ~23 minutes per wallpaper). It builds one
> independent panel per screen and repaints the joins, because a long outpainting chain like the one below drifts:
> in our 15-panel test, each extension inherited the previous one's flaws and the second half turned into a blocky
> mosaic. The manual chain is fine for a few panels.

Work at **~1 MP scale**, where the models are sharpest, and scale up only at the end:
target strip **13,440 × 768** (= 10 × 1344 × 768, 17.5 : 1). That is close enough to 19,200 × 1,080 (17.8 : 1)
that the 1.6 % stretch is invisible; for a room with gaps, scale the strip width to the hint's aspect.

| Step | Canvas | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Hero panel | 1344 × 768 | Z-Image: style prefix + beat 1. Generate 8–16 seeds, keep the best. This panel sets the style for everything. |
| 2. Extend ×12 | 1344 × 768 | Crop the **right 336 px** of the strip so far → `Pad Image for Outpainting` right **1008**, feathering 40 → Flux Fill with style prefix + the next beat → keep the new 1008 px and append them. 1344 + 12 × 1008 = 13,440. |
| 3. Close the ring | 1344 × 768 | See §4.4. |
| 4. Fix seams | — | View the whole strip at 25 %; inpaint any visible joins (mask ~200 px wide on the join, Flux Fill). |
| 5. Upscale | 19,200 × 1,080 | §4.5 |

Extension tips:
- **Always include the style prefix.** It keeps colours and rendering consistent from panel to panel.
- Describe **only what should appear in the new area**: "…a ramen shop with paper lanterns", not the whole street again.
- If perspective drifts (horizon climbing), redo that extension with "horizon line at 60 % of the image height" in the prompt.
- Keep every intermediate strip (`strip_01.png`, `strip_02.png`, …), so a bad extension costs one step, not the evening.

## 4. ComfyUI workflow details

### 4.1 Hero panel (Z-Image Turbo)

```
Load Diffusion Model (z_image_turbo_bf16) ─┐
Load CLIP (qwen_3_4b, type lumina2) ─ CLIP Text Encode (prompt) ─ KSampler (8 steps, CFG 1, res_multistep, simple)
Empty Latent Image 1344 × 768 ─────────────┘                          └ VAE Decode (ae) ─ Save Image
```

### 4.2 Extension (Flux Fill)

```
Load Image (strip so far) ─ ImageCrop (x = width − 336, w 336, h 768)
   ─ Pad Image for Outpainting (right 1008, feathering 40) ─┬ image
                                                            └ mask
DualCLIPLoader (t5xxl + clip_l) ─ CLIP Text Encode (style prefix + beat) ─ FluxGuidance 30
InpaintModelConditioning (positive, negative, vae, pixels = padded image, mask) ─ KSampler (20–28 steps, CFG 1, euler, normal, denoise 1.0)
   ─ VAE Decode ─ ImageCrop (x = 336, w 1008) ─ Save Image  →  append to the strip
```

Add a `Differential Diffusion` node on the model for softer joins. Appending is easiest outside ComfyUI
(GIMP / Krita / Photoshop: extend the canvas, paste at x = old width), or with `Image Stitch` in ComfyUI.

### 4.3 Seam repair

Crop a 1344 × 768 window centred on the seam, mask a ~200 px vertical band over it, run Flux Fill with the style
prefix, and paste the window back.

### 4.4 Closing the ring (the left/right edges meet)

The classic texture "offset" trick:

1. Cut the last **672 px** and the first **672 px** of the strip.
2. Put them side by side, **end piece on the left**, into a 1344 × 768 image. The ugly join is now in the middle.
3. Mask a **448 px** band centred on the join and run Flux Fill with the style prefix (plus "seamless continuation").
4. Split the result at x = 672 again and paste the halves back over the end and the start of the strip.

Check it by viewing the strip doubled side by side. The join must be invisible.

### 4.5 Upscale to the room size

1. `Upscale Image (using Model)` with 4x-UltraSharp → 53,760 × 3,072. This is too big to edit, so tile it if your RAM
   complains: split the strip into 4 parts with 200 px overlap and upscale each.
2. `Upscale Image` (lanczos) **down** to the size the hint shows (e.g. 19,200 × 1,080).
3. Optional detail pass: Ultimate SD Upscale with Z-Image Turbo, denoise 0.2–0.3, tile 1024, seam fix on.
4. Save as **JPG quality 92**. The players load the file once per scene; a 20k × 1k JPG is ~5–10 MB, transferred
   once per client and cached.

## 5. Hiding "BiBaBu"

From obvious to subtle:

1. **Signs and objects (easy):** neon signs, a blimp banner, graffiti, a ship's name, lantern lettering. Put the text
   in quotes in the prompt. If a letter comes out wrong, fix it with Qwen-Image-Edit: "change the sign text to "BiBaBu"".
2. **Spread over the room:** one letter per screen pair (B · i · B · a · B · u over 10 screens), e.g. constellations
   or lit windows forming one letter each. Only people walking around the room see the whole word.
3. **Illusion lettering (hardest, best wow effect):** a white "BiBaBu" on black, as wide as the whole strip, used as a
   ControlNet input (QR Code Monster v2 on SDXL, strength 0.9–1.3, end at 0.6–0.8). Generate the strip in pieces
   with the matching slice of the mask for each piece. The word appears in mountain ridges, clouds or city lights
   when you step back.
4. **Post-production (reliable):** write the text in Krita/GIMP and blend it in (Overlay / Soft Light, 20–40 %
   opacity, follow the perspective). This is no shame: it is how most wallpaper artists do it.

## 8. The ready-made workflow (FLUX.2 Klein 9B, 12 GB VRAM)

> **Superseded by the script in §9** (2026-10-18) for finished wallpapers: native 1920 px per screen and no hard
> seams. The workflow stays useful for trying prompts interactively.

Set up and tested on 2026-10-16 (RTX 4070 Ti, 12 GB VRAM, 32 GB RAM, ComfyUI 0.41). It does route B in one Run and
uses only models that are already installed: `flux-2-klein-base-9b-fp8`, `qwen_3_8b_fp8mixed` and `flux2-vae`.

**Files:**

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `ComfyUI/user/default/workflows/Panorama.json` | Deep Space (§6.1), open "Panorama" in ComfyUI |
| `…/workflows/Panorama - Neon Harbour.json`, `Panorama - Mountains.json` | §6.2 and §6.3, with logo beats of their own |
| `…/workflows/Panorama - Fantasy Realm.json` | High fantasy at golden hour: castle, dwarven gate with the lettering, dwarf statue with the Bart, tavern with the Bier sign |
| `…/workflows/Panorama - Pixel Quest.json` | 16-bit platformer world, snapped to a true pixel grid (see *Pixel art* below) |
| `ComfyUI/custom_nodes/panorama_tone_match.py` | Four small nodes the workflow needs (copy kept in `tools/comfyui/`) |
| `tools/comfyui/gen_panorama_workflow.py` | Regenerates the workflow, e.g. for a different number of screens |

**Use it:**

1. Open the **Panorama** workflow (or one of the other two). The green group on the left holds every prompt: the
   **style prefix**, the **panel layout** line, **beats 1–10** (one per screen) and the **seam** prompt.
2. Edit the prompts and press **Run**: 10 panels + 10 seams ≈ 20 sampler runs ≈ 23 minutes. Start ComfyUI with
   `F:\ComfyUI\run_nvidia_gpu_panorama.bat` (async offload and pinned memory off; with them on, long runs aborted).
3. Results go to `ComfyUI/output/panorama/`: `<concept>_strip_*.png` (13,440 × 768) and `<concept>_room_*.png`
   (19,200 × 1,097 lanczos, for WaBiBaBuSy's Panorama background).
4. **Club logo:** beats 3, 5, 7 and 9 get a logo image as a reference (the pink "Logo references" group): the
   "BiBaBu" lettering, the **Bart**, the **Bier** mug and the **Busen**, each turned into an object of the scene
   (Deep Space: station sign, constellation, nebula, two moons; Neon Harbour: bar neon, mural, brewery neon, boutique
   neon; Mountains: carved hut sign, snow ridges, beer-garden sign, two hilltops). The images are
   `ComfyUI/input/bibabu_*.png`, rendered white-on-black from the club's SVGs; swap them in the LoadImage nodes. The
   beat text must say what to take from the reference ("…exactly like the reference line drawing").
5. To change one screen, edit its beat and Run again: only that panel and its two seams are re-rendered (every panel
   and seam has its own fixed seed; ComfyUI caches the rest). For a different take, change that panel's **Seed** node.

**Regenerate** (ComfyUI running with the custom nodes, or `--object-info <saved /object_info JSON>`):

```
F:\ComfyUI\python_embeded\python.exe tools\comfyui\gen_panorama_workflow.py --out F:\ComfyUI\ComfyUI\user\default\workflows\Panorama.json
```

`--concept deep_space|neon_harbour|mountains|fantasy_realm|pixel_quest`, `--pixel-size 2|3|4|5|6|8` (0 = off), `--colors`, `--screens 2..12` (one panel per screen, 1344 px each), `--steps`,
`--prefix`. More than 12 screens exceeds ComfyUI's 16,384 px image limit. For two rows of 10, make **one strip per row**
and put them side by side: the join between them lands in the turn gap, which no screen shows.

**Pixel art.** Image models only draw *fake* pixel art: uneven pixel sizes, anti-aliased edges, smeared colours (a
dedicated pixel model or LoRA helps the style, but cannot do the logo references and seams). The workflow therefore
snaps the finished strip to a real grid: down to N × 1920 / px × 1080 / px with `area`, one shared palette with
`ImageQuantize` (48 colours, no dither), then nearest-neighbour × px back up to **exactly N × 1920 × 1080**, so every
screen gets its slice 1:1 and the pixels stay crisp. `pixel_quest` uses 6 px; any concept can use `--pixel-size`. If
the room canvas is wider than N × 1920 (bezel gaps), the player scales the slices and the pixels soften slightly.

**How it works, and why** (each point was a visible defect in a test run):

| Step | Detail | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Panels | One independent Klein text-to-image panel per screen, 1344 × 768, 20 steps, CFG 5, euler, **own seed** | A left-to-right outpainting chain (each panel continuing the previous one) degraded into a blocky mosaic after ~6 panels: every step inherited and amplified the last one's flaws. A shared seed also repeated the same cloud shapes in every panel. |
| Layout | Style prefix pins the composition (nebula band / horizon at a fixed height); the panel-only layout line keeps each beat's subject away from the edges | Independent panels still line up; the edges are repainted by the seams anyway. "A small centred subject" made Klein invent one (a lone human figure). |
| Seams | Every join, including the ring join (panel 10 → panel 1): right half of one panel + left half of the next, a **576 px grey band** in the middle, masked sampling (`SetLatentNoiseMask`) | Bridges two unrelated compositions and covers Klein's darkened panel rims (vignette) |
| Seam latents | Slices of the panels' *sampled* latents (`PanoramaLatentSlice`, `PanoramaLatentConcat`), not re-encoded pixels | Re-encoded pixels decode up to 2.5× darker next to sampled latents (the VAE decoder normalises over the whole image) |
| Seam reference | The two halves with a **grey band** in between as `ReferenceLatent`, prompt "Fill the empty grey band in the middle…", plus a seam negative ("planets, …, human figure, text") | Klein is an edit model: a reference with the seam in it was reproduced faithfully, seam included; without the negative it copied objects into the band |
| Band only | `PanoramaToneMatch` + `ImageCompositeMasked`: only the band (soft edges) comes from the seam, outside it the original panel pixels | Decoded kept parts differ slightly per seam, which showed as a hard line where two seam windows meet (every panel centre) |
| No Differential Diffusion | Plain noise mask | In its last steps Differential Diffusion repaints the kept context too |
| Upscale | `ImageScaleBy` lanczos ×1.4286 | Image nodes stop at 16,384 px, so the room size is reached by scale factor (1.6 % taller than 1080; WaBiBaBuSy stretches it to the canvas) |

## 9. Native-resolution script with clean seams (`tools/comfyui/panorama.py`)

Added 2026-10-18. Same idea as §8 (one independent Klein panel per screen, every join repainted with both neighbours
as context, ring join included), but driven from Python through the ComfyUI API instead of one big workflow:

| | §8 workflow | §9 script |
|---|---|---|
| Panel size | 1344 × 768, lanczos ×1.43 up | **1920 × 1088 per screen, no upscale** (cropped to 1080): every screen pixel is generated, much more detail |
| Room width limit | 12 screens (16,384 px image nodes) | none (assembled in numpy) |
| Seam blend | 576 px band, 24 px blur, per-column tone gain | 768 px band, 96 px ramp, **per-row colour correction**: the band's difference to the panels is measured just outside both band edges, per row, and interpolated across the band |
| Pixel art | 6 px grid, 48 colours | 4 px grid, 64 colours (more detail survives) |
| Re-render one piece | change its seed node | delete `panel_03.png` / `seam_03.png` and re-run |
| Time (10 screens) | ~23 min | ~50 min (panel ~90 s, seam ~200 s) |

**Why the old wallpapers had hard vertical lines** (worst in Pixel Quest): the repainted band came back in a slightly
different colour than the panels around it, and the 24 px blur turned that step into a visible line at both band
edges. The per-column tone gain could not fix it because the difference changes with height (sky vs ground). The
per-row correction removes the step exactly at the band edges; inside the band the content is the model's own.

**Run** (ComfyUI running, `run_nvidia_gpu_panorama.bat`):

```
F:\ComfyUI\python_embeded\python.exe tools\comfyui\panorama.py --concept lan_party
```

Concepts live in `tools/comfyui/panorama_concepts.py` (style prefix, one beat per screen, logo references, seam
prompts): `lan_party` (pixel art, new), `pixel_quest`, `neon_harbour`, `mountains`, `fantasy_realm`. Options:
`--screens`, `--pixel-size`, `--colors`, `--band`, `--ramp`, `--seed`, `--only p3,s3`, `--reseed 3`, `--match`, `--out`.

**Seam colour match** (`--match`): `edge` (default) compares the band with the panels just outside it, so it removes the
VAE's tone shift and never tints. When the model paints a *different* colour inside the band (Neon Harbour: grey sky
next to a teal one), `inner` compares the panels with the band's full-strength core instead and spreads the change
across the band; on scenes where the two sides show different things in the same rows (Pixel Quest: sky next to a
hill) it tints the band in visible rectangles, so use it only where it helps. It only re-assembles: no GPU.

**A lighter box around a logo subject** (seen once in Pixel Quest): the reference image leaked its card shape.
Move `panel_03.png`, `seam_02.png`, `seam_03.png` away and run with `--reseed 3`. Output:
`F:\ComfyUI\ComfyUI\output\panorama2\<concept>\<concept>_room.jpg|png` plus a 1/8 preview.

Prompt lessons from the test runs:
- **Flat side view, no vanishing point** for anything with lines (tables, streets): every panel otherwise invents its
  own perspective and the seams have to bend lines into each other.
- Pin the **scale of people** in the style prefix ("every person about one fifth of the image height"), or panels
  disagree on how big things are.
- "Rich tiny details: …" with a list of small things in the style prefix fills every panel; Klein at 2 MP draws them.

## 10. BiBaBu logo styles (`tools/comfyui/logo_styles.py`)

Klein 9B gets the white line drawing of a logo part (`ComfyUI/input/bibabu_bier|bart|busen.png`) as reference image
and a style prompt ("Redraw the beard from the reference line drawing as a hand-carved wooden sculpture. Keep exactly
the same silhouette…"), on a flat white (or black) background. The script keys the background out (flood fill from
the border, plus enclosed areas big enough to be holes such as the cups or the mug handle; the neon style keeps its glow as soft alpha) and saves a trimmed transparent
sprite, longest side 512 px.

20 styles: gold, chrome, pixel, voxel, lego, stainedglass, patch, sticker, graffiti, lowpoly, plush, wood, ice,
origami, gingerbread, gummy, balloon, tattoo, popart, neon. Add a line to `STYLES` for more.

